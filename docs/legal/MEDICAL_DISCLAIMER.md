## 1. No Medical Advice or Primary Diagnosis

TrustFed is an algorithmic research and educational decision-support prototype. **It is NOT a medical device, diagnostic system, clinical tool, or healthcare provider.**

1. **Informational Heuristics Only**: All differential diagnosis probabilities, suggested causes, and recommended remedies are computational outputs derived from public reference benchmarks (e.g. UCI ML repository) and synthetic clinical profiles ($N=1,080$). They do NOT represent a certified medical diagnosis.
2. **Never Substitute Professional Judgment**: Nothing contained within this service is intended to substitute for the clinical acumen, diagnostic testing, physical examination, or professional judgment of a licensed physician or registered nurse.
3. **No Patient-Physician Relationship**: Interacting with TrustFed does NOT create or constitute a doctor-patient, provider-patient, or confidential healthcare relationship.

## 2. Emergency Medical Guidance

> [!WARNING]
> **IF YOU OR SOMEONE AROUND YOU IS EXPERIENCING A MEDICAL EMERGENCY, DO NOT RELY ON THIS SERVICE.**  
> Call emergency medical services or go to the nearest emergency department immediately:
> - **India**: `112` (National Emergency Number)
> - **United States / Canada**: `911`
> - **United Kingdom**: `999`
> - **European Union**: `112`
> 
> Critical emergency warning signs include, but are not limited to:
> - Sudden severe chest pain or pressure radiating to the arm, neck, or jaw
> - Sudden shortness of breath, gasping, or severe wheezing
> - Sudden facial drooping, arm or leg weakness, or slurred speech (FAST signs)
> - High pyrexia in infants under 1 year of age ($\ge 38.5^\circ\text{C} / 101.3^\circ\text{F}$)
> - Stiff neck accompanied by high fever and severe headache
> - Severe blood loss, continuous vomiting, or inability to retain fluids with dizziness

## 3. All Heuristics Labeled "Pending Physician Review"

In accordance with our safety architecture, all numerical vitals thresholds:
- Hypoxia ($\text{SpO}_2 < 92.0\%$)
- Tachycardia ($\text{HR} \ge 120.0\text{ bpm}$)
- Severe Bradycardia ($\text{HR} < 40.0\text{ bpm}$)
- Hypotension ($\text{Systolic BP} < 90.0\text{ mmHg}$)
- Hypertensive Crisis ($\text{Systolic BP} \ge 180.0\text{ mmHg}$ or $\text{Diastolic BP} \ge 120.0\text{ mmHg}$)
- Infant Pyrexia ($\text{Temp} \ge 38.5^\circ\text{C}$ in age $\le 1\text{ year}$)

are conservative heuristic starting points labeled **"Pending Physician Review"**. They have not undergone randomized controlled clinical trials and must be reviewed and customized by an attending healthcare practitioner.
