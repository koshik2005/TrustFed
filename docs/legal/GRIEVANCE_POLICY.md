## 3. How to Submit a Grievance

To submit a grievance or report a concern, please send an email to `grievance-officer@trustfed.ai` with the following details:
1. **Full Name and Affiliation** (e.g. Student, Physician, Researcher).
2. **Contact Email Address and Phone Number**.
3. **Clear Description of the Grievance**:
   - Specific URL, feature, or algorithmic output in question.
   - Date and approximate time of the encounter.
   - Nature of concern (e.g. Algorithmic bias, data handling, privacy question, medical accuracy feedback, technical error).
4. **Supporting Documentation** (if applicable: screenshots, anonymized input parameters).
5. **Statement of Truth**: A brief declaration confirming that the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

---

## 4. Timelines for Resolution

In accordance with standard digital intermediary rules in India:
1. **Acknowledgment**: The Grievance Officer shall acknowledge receipt of your complaint within **twenty-four (24) to forty-eight (48) hours**.
2. **Investigation & Resolution**: The complaint shall be investigated by our technical and clinical review team, and a formal response or resolution will be provided within **fifteen (15) calendar days** from the date of receipt.
